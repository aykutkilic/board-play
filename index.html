        Hands’ pace

        Men: drag a man – your hand takes it up, follows and puts it down –, or tap it and then where it goes. Let go elsewhere and it goes back.

        The clock: your move counts when you have pressed your clock: tap the clock on the table, the dial at the bottom, or Space. Until then your time runs. Black starts the clock before the first move. A flag that falls ends the game. Mate and stalemate end it at once.

        Dice: tap Roll (or the dice, or Space); or hold it to shake the dice, and flick up to throw: where and how fast you flick is how they fly. There is one pair: a second after your throw the other player takes the dice; what you threw stays on the screen while you play. Done (Space) ends your turn. ↶ takes a man back before that.

        Doubling cube: before your throw, Double – the other side takes or drops.

        Cards: drag a card (or a run of them), or tap it and it goes where it fits: home, on to another, to a cell. In Klondike tap the stock (or Space) to turn. In bridge tap a card of yours to play it – and dummy’s, when you declare; the calls are on the pad.

        Look round: drag beside the board (or the right mouse button, two fingers), wheel or pinch zooms, double-click beside the board straightens the view.

        Keys: F2 / C camera, Space clock · roll · done, U take back, L the log, Esc the menu.
